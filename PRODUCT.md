# Parcelith

Inferred from the client brief and the shipped product; not interviewed.

## What it is

A multi-store SaaS for launching, customising and selling physical products without holding inventory. Businesses, creators and agencies source products from manufacturers, dropshippers and print-on-demand suppliers, configure them with artwork and options, preview mockups, and sell them through branded storefronts. Corporate gifting, an AI assistant that proposes changes for review, localized checkout and audit logs sit on top.

## Who uses it

- **Store teams and agencies** running one or many stores: catalog, pricing, approvals, orders and fulfilment exceptions.
- **Companies** sending gifts to employees and clients through private catalogs and recipient lists.
- **Shoppers** on the storefronts, who only see products, prices, delivery and checkout.
- **Platform admins** managing suppliers, plans and audit.

## Register

Product UI for the workspace, brand register for the marketing site. Both share one calm zinc-and-teal system (see DESIGN.md).

## Principles

- Clear and fast. Short, specific copy in the style of Shopify, Stripe and Linear; no internals shown to users.
- Accessible by default: text meets WCAG AA (4.5:1) on every surface it sits on, including the grey bands.
- Trustworthy over flashy. Changes to live products are reviewed before they publish.
- Localized: shopper-facing copy, money, dates and countries follow the store's language.
